# Code of conduct

## The short version

This is a study resource. People arrive at it knowing nothing about the subject, which is the entire point of "zero to hero". Treat a beginner's question as the reason the repository exists rather than as noise.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient with questions that seem basic. Everyone reading this was at the start once, and most people here are studying around a job.
- Critique content, not people. "This IAM example grants more than it needs" is useful. "Whoever wrote this does not understand IAM" is not.
- Assume good faith about errors. A wrong fact in a cert guide is usually a vendor that changed something, not carelessness.
- Cite sources when you correct something. A vendor documentation link settles an argument faster than an assertion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including anything targeting someone's background, identity, or level of experience.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Posting verbatim exam questions from any vendor. Beyond being a license violation, it undermines the credential everyone here is working towards. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Using issues or pull requests primarily to advertise a product or service.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, **[@PatrickWiloak](https://github.com/PatrickWiloak)**, by opening a private report through [GitHub's reporting flow](https://github.com/PatrickWiloak/cloud-data-ai-security-zero-to-hero/security/advisories/new) or by direct message on any of the channels linked from the [README](./README.md).

Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit, delete, or decline any contribution, and may block an account, for behavior that breaches this document.
